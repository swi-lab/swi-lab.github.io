## Prospective students

**Please submit the [Prospective Student Interest Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSevkyzei9QM-D7Be4mz1CaxUYGuBLt6in83I0SM3M6e8V-Q2Q/viewform) instead of emailing Jiayun Wang directly.** All inquiries about joining the lab are reviewed through this form.

- **PhD applicants.** Apply through [Georgia Tech graduate admissions](https://grad.gatech.edu/admissions) and name Jiayun Wang as a potential advisor in your statement of purpose. Note that we typically accept students from CSE, CS and ML programs. You are also welcome to submit the [interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSevkyzei9QM-D7Be4mz1CaxUYGuBLt6in83I0SM3M6e8V-Q2Q/viewform).
- **Georgia Tech graduate and undergraduate students.** Submit the [interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSevkyzei9QM-D7Be4mz1CaxUYGuBLt6in83I0SM3M6e8V-Q2Q/viewform) with your research interests, CV and (unofficial) transcript. A commitment of at least six months and a goal to publish are required. Students from computing/computer science and other disciplines are welcome. We will follow up when your interests align with a project in the lab.
- **Visitors and research interns.** If you study at another institution and would like to do research with us, submit the [interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSevkyzei9QM-D7Be4mz1CaxUYGuBLt6in83I0SM3M6e8V-Q2Q/viewform) with your research interests, CV and (unofficial) transcript. A commitment of at least six months and a goal to publish are required. We will review your submission and get in touch if there is a suitable opportunity.
- **Postdocs.** At this moment, we do not have postdoc positions available. Check back for more information.

Due to the large volume of inquiries, we are unable to reply to every submission—apologies!
